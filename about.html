License Review

How we check licenses.

We check each provider’s license against the issuing board’s records. We recheck it annually. If we find that a license has lapsed, we pause the profile until we confirm that it’s active again. See our sources.

See what we review

We review each provider’s record on their licensing board’s official lookup. Records vary by board.

  • License number, type and status
  • Issue and expiration dates
  • Disciplinary actions, in-state and in other states
  • Pending charges
  • Other public actions or administrative fines
  • Malpractice judgments and settlements
  • Criminal convictions reported by the courts
  • Training and board certification

A patient’s guide

How to choose an aesthetic provider

Seven things to look at before you book.

  1. 01

    Check their license

    On BeautyJar, this is done for you.

  2. 02

    Ask who will treat you

    If someone else will perform the treatment, ask for their full name and professional credential.

  3. 03

    Ask about their experience

    Where did they train for this treatment, and how often do they perform it?

  4. 04

    See whether their approach fits your goals

    Ask to see examples of their work and discuss what they’d recommend for you.

  5. 05

    Use the consultation

    Ask about risks, downtime, follow-up, and whom to contact if you have a concern afterward.

  6. 06

    Get the full price

    Ask whether pricing is per unit, syringe, or session, and what the total includes.

  7. 07

    Take your time

    Give yourself room to think it over and ask any follow-up questions before booking.

A license confirms a provider’s professional status at the time it’s checked. It doesn’t measure skill or guarantee results. A consultation is your chance to talk about your goals, ask about their experience and decide whether the practice feels right for you.

How do I know if a provider is right for me?

Start with their page: their training, their approach and the treatments they focus on. Every license is confirmed, though a license doesn’t measure skill or guarantee results. A consultation is your chance to ask questions and see whether the practice feels right for you.

Where is BeautyJar available?

BeautyJar is open to licensed providers across the United States, starting in Washington, DC, Maryland and Northern Virginia. International providers are coming soon.

Regulation

The rules are changing.

States are tightening who can perform aesthetic treatments and how they’re supervised. Texas (Jenifer’s Law), Rhode Island, Indiana and California have all passed new laws since 2025. BeautyJar tracks these changes and confirms every provider’s license.
