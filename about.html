About BeautyJar

A better way to find aesthetic care.

A directory for finding and researching aesthetic providers.

Our Mission

Better information. Easier discovery.

BeautyJar was created to make the growing world of non-surgical aesthetics easier to navigate. From injectables and laser treatments to body contouring, hair restoration and aesthetic recovery, finding the right provider can be just as important as choosing the treatment itself.

BeautyJar brings provider discovery, treatment information and aesthetic trends together in one easy-to-explore place.

What You’ll Find

A focused aesthetics directory.

Explore providers and practices across Injectables, Skin + Laser, Body Contouring, Hair Restoration and Aesthetic Recovery. Browse profiles, learn about services and connect directly with practices in the US and globally.

Credential Checked

What “Credential Checked” means.

1. Credential verification

Before publishing, we check applicable credentials against the right licensing or registration source for that provider.

2. Clear credential status

We confirm information at the time it’s reviewed. This does not rank clinical skill, guarantee outcomes, or determine fit for any individual.

Credential + Research Sources

The governing bodies behind the checks.

Credentialing rules vary by profession and location. BeautyJar references recognized licensing, registration, certification and professional bodies appropriate to the provider and jurisdiction. We keep these sources visible so consumers can understand where credential information comes from.
