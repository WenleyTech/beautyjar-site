What cookies are

Cookies are small data files that websites can place on a browser or device. Websites may also use related technologies, such as local storage, pixels and server logs, to operate, secure and understand use of a site.

How BeautyJar may use these technologies

BeautyJar may use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Security — to help protect the site and identify potentially harmful or unusual activity;
  • Performance — to understand whether pages and site features are working properly; and
  • Analytics — if enabled, to understand general site traffic and how visitors interact with BeautyJar.

Essential technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for hosting, security, network management or other basic website functions. These technologies may operate automatically when you visit BeautyJar.

Analytics

BeautyJar may use analytics tools to understand general patterns such as page visits, referral sources, device or browser type and site performance.

Your choices

If BeautyJar introduces technologies that require additional consent or opt-out choices under applicable law, we will provide those choices as appropriate.
