BeautyJar FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Information for people exploring aesthetic care and professionals considering a BeautyJar membership.

Finding Care

What is BeautyJar?

BeautyJar is a directory for discovering aesthetic professionals by treatment, specialty and location. Provider pages are designed to make professional credentials, services, practice information and booking options easier to understand.

Is BeautyJar free for consumers?

Yes. BeautyJar is free to search and browse.

What does “Credential Checked” mean?

BeautyJar reviews selected professional credentials and practice information using applicable public licensing, registration and professional sources. The credential status reflects the information available when the review was completed.

A Credential Checked designation does not constitute an endorsement, guarantee treatment quality or replace a consumer’s own evaluation of whether a provider is appropriate for their needs.

Does BeautyJar recommend or endorse providers?

No. BeautyJar provides information to support provider discovery. Consumers should independently evaluate a provider’s qualifications, experience, services and suitability before receiving care.

How is provider information obtained?

Provider and practice information may be submitted directly by the provider or an authorized practice representative. BeautyJar may review selected information against applicable public and professional sources before publication.

How do I contact or book with a provider?

Where available, provider pages include a direct booking link, practice website, telephone number or other contact information. Appointments are made directly with the provider or practice, not through BeautyJar.

How can I report information that may be inaccurate?

Please email hello@beautyjar.com with the provider’s name, page link and the information you believe may be inaccurate. BeautyJar will review the report and may contact the provider or consult appropriate public sources before making a change.

For Providers

Who can join BeautyJar?

BeautyJar accepts qualified aesthetic professionals and practices whose services and professional standing meet the directory’s listing requirements. Eligibility may depend on the provider’s profession, services, credentials and jurisdiction.

What does a BeautyJar membership provide?

BeautyJar gives aesthetic professionals a dedicated, shareable page designed to help prospective patients understand who they are, what they offer and how to connect with their practice.

Providers can be discovered by treatment, specialty and location. Their page can include credentials, services, practice details, professional images and a direct booking link. BeautyJar also reviews selected professional and practice information before publication and throughout the year.

What is the difference between Profile and Spotlight?

Profile provides a polished professional page with the provider’s biography, credentials, services, practice information, images and booking details.

Spotlight provides a more detailed editorial-style presentation with additional images and expanded information about the provider’s background, approach, professional focus and practice. Spotlight also includes enhanced placement within BeautyJar and eligibility for selected BeautyJar editorial and social features.

Is membership for an individual provider or an entire practice?

Each membership represents one individual provider. If several professionals work at the same practice, each provider requires a separate listing. Their pages may reference the same practice, location and booking information.

What happens after I purchase a membership?

After payment, the provider receives a link to submit professional credentials, practice information, written content and images. BeautyJar reviews the submitted information, prepares the appropriate Profile or Spotlight page and sends a private preview for factual review before publication.

What information will I need to submit?

Providers are asked for the professional and practice information needed to review and prepare their listing, including credentials, license details, services, biography, practice location, contact information, booking links and professional images.

How long does it take for my page to be published?

Page preparation begins after BeautyJar receives the completed onboarding form and the information needed for review. Timing may vary depending on the completeness of the submission and whether clarification or additional information is required.

Will I review my page before it is published?

Yes. BeautyJar sends a private preview so the provider can review factual information before publication.

How long will my Profile or Spotlight remain published?

A provider’s page remains published while the membership is active and the provider and practice information continue to meet BeautyJar’s listing requirements. If the membership is cancelled, the page may be removed at the end of the applicable billing period.

How often is my information reviewed?

BeautyJar reviews submitted professional and practice information before publication and rechecks selected credentials and listing details throughout the year. Providers must notify BeautyJar when their license, practice location, services or other published information changes.

Can I update my page after publication?

Yes. Providers may request reasonable updates to credentials, services, locations, booking details, biographies and images by emailing hello@beautyjar.com. BeautyJar may review supporting information before publishing certain changes.

Can I upgrade from Profile to Spotlight?

Yes. A Profile member may upgrade to Spotlight. BeautyJar will request the additional written information and images needed to prepare the expanded Spotlight page.

Can I share my BeautyJar page?

Yes. Every published provider receives a dedicated page link that can be shared through the provider’s website, social media, email signature and professional materials.

Can BeautyJar edit my submitted biography?

BeautyJar may edit submitted content for clarity, length, formatting and consistency without intentionally changing its factual meaning. Providers receive a private preview before publication.

Who owns the photographs and materials I submit?

Providers retain ownership of their submitted materials and authorize BeautyJar to use them for their listing and related BeautyJar promotion. Providers must own or have permission to submit and publish all photographs, logos and written materials provided.

Does BeautyJar guarantee appointments or leads?

BeautyJar is designed to increase professional visibility and make it easier for prospective patients to discover and connect with providers. Because patient decisions depend on many factors, BeautyJar does not guarantee a specific number of inquiries or appointments.

What happens if BeautyJar cannot approve my listing?

If BeautyJar cannot approve a paid listing following its review, the membership will be cancelled and the initial payment refunded.

How do I cancel my membership?

Providers may request cancellation by emailing hello@beautyjar.com. The cancellation will apply according to the membership terms, and the provider’s page may be removed at the end of the applicable billing period.

What happens if I change practices?

Please notify BeautyJar before or promptly after the change. BeautyJar can review the new practice information and update the provider’s page once the relevant details have been confirmed.
