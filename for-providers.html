How it works

Four simple steps.

After checkout, you’ll provide your professional credentials, practice details, profile content and images. BeautyJar verifies the information before publication.

01

Choose a Membership

Select Profile or Spotlight and complete checkout.

02

Complete Onboarding

Submit your credentials, practice details, profile content and images. Spotlight members receive a separate video upload link and video guidelines in their onboarding email.

03

Verify

We confirm your credentials and practice information. If we cannot approve the listing, we cancel the membership and refund the initial payment.

04

Publish

We prepare your page and send a private preview. Most pages are published within 7 business days after we receive a complete submission.
