Effective November 11, 2026 · Version 2026-11-11

About these terms

These Founding Provider Terms apply to a professional who applies for a complimentary BeautyJar Profile through the BeautyJar Founding Provider offer. BeautyJar is operated by Wenley Technologies LLC.

By submitting the Founding Provider application, you agree to these Founding Provider Terms, the Provider Terms and the BeautyJar Terms of Service.

A Founding Provider Profile is a complimentary membership that never expires. No payment, subscription or credit card is required.

The Founding Provider offer

The offer is open to licensed physicians, nurse practitioners, physician assistants and registered nurses who practice in the District of Columbia, Maryland or Northern Virginia (including Arlington, Fairfax, Loudoun and Prince William counties and the cities of Alexandria, Fairfax, Falls Church, Manassas and Manassas Park).

Founding spots are limited to the first 50 providers approved by BeautyJar, in the order applications are approved. Submitting an application does not reserve a spot or guarantee approval or publication. Applications that are incomplete, ineligible or not approved do not count toward the 50.

BeautyJar may close the offer at any time, including before 50 providers are approved. Applications received after the offer closes, or after all spots are filled, may be offered a paid membership or placed on a waitlist. BeautyJar will tell you whether a founding spot is available before your page is published.

Founding Provider mark

Each approved Founding Provider’s page displays a Founding Provider mark for as long as the founding listing remains active.

One provider per listing

Each Founding Provider listing applies to one individual provider. A practice with several eligible professionals may submit a separate application for each provider, and each counts as one of the 50 spots. A founding listing is personal to the named provider and may not be transferred, sold or assigned to another professional.

The complimentary status follows the named provider if the provider changes practices, subject to updated information, license review and continued eligibility. It does not transfer to the provider’s former or new colleagues, employer or practice.

Included page features

A Founding Provider Profile includes the same professional page features as a paid Profile: a searchable page, the provider’s license and professional background, biography, treatments and optional starting prices, new-patient and virtual consultation status, languages, years in aesthetics, consultation fee, medical director where applicable, practice locations, payment and financing information, headshot and direct booking link. Optional details appear only when supplied.

Spotlight features are not included in the Founding Provider offer. A Founding Provider may upgrade to Spotlight as a paid membership at the price then offered. Featured placement is paid visibility and is not a clinical endorsement. The Edit is independently selected and inclusion is not a membership or Founding Provider benefit.

How long your Profile stays complimentary

A Founding Provider Profile never expires and Founding Providers never pay a membership fee for it. This means BeautyJar will not charge the Founding Provider a monthly membership fee for the founding Profile for as long as BeautyJar continues to operate the applicable directory service and the provider remains eligible, in good standing and compliant with these terms.

The offer has no cash value and does not create a right to payment, reimbursement, substitution, resale or transfer.

You authorize BeautyJar to compare submitted professional and practice information against applicable public, licensing, registration and professional sources.

This does not certify clinical skill, guarantee outcomes or replace a patient’s own evaluation. Practice details, prices, payment options and training are provider-reported.
