About Dr. Abby

Dr. Abby Sample is a board-certified dermatologist and founder of Sample Dermatology & Aesthetics in Bethesda. Her practice focuses on skin health, laser treatments and cosmetic dermatology.

She works with patients seeking thoughtful treatment plans, clearer skin and natural-looking aesthetic results.
