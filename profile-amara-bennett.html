About Amara

Care built around the individual.

Amara Bennett, DNP, FNP-C, is a Family Nurse Practitioner and founder of Bennett Aesthetics + Wellness in Atlanta. Her practice focuses on aesthetic medicine and skin-focused care, with an emphasis on individualized treatment planning and natural-looking results.

She considers goals, health history, lifestyle and long-term maintenance before recommending a plan. For aesthetic treatments, she favors restraint and results that still look like the patient.
