The Expert Advantage

How qualified providers stand out in medical aesthetics, and what today’s patients look for before they book.

Published November 2026 · 13 pages · Free PDF

About this report

Medical aesthetics has become a mainstream part of how millions of people care for themselves, and the industry is maturing. Practices are growing by deepening relationships with the patients they already have, while patients have become careful researchers who compare credentials, read reviews and look for natural results that last.

This report brings together the latest industry data on that shift: where growth is coming from, what patients look for when they choose a provider, what the U.S. can learn from South Korea, and five ways the best providers make their expertise visible.
