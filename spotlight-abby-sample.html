About Dr. Abby

Her approach starts with the individual.

Dr. Abby Sample is a board-certified dermatologist and founder of Sample Dermatology & Aesthetics in Bethesda. Her practice focuses on skin health, laser treatments and cosmetic dermatology, with an emphasis on thoughtful treatment plans and natural-looking results.

She considers skin history, goals, lifestyle and long-term maintenance before recommending a plan. For aesthetic treatments, she favors restraint and results that still look like the patient.
