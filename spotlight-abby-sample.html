About Dr. Abby

Dr. Abby Sample is a board-certified dermatologist and the founder of Sample Dermatology & Aesthetics in Bethesda. Her practice focuses on skin health, laser treatments and cosmetic dermatology, with an emphasis on thoughtful treatment plans and natural-looking results.

Her profile is presented here as a BeautyJar sample to demonstrate the richer storytelling, imagery and professional positioning included with a Spotlight profile.

Her Approach

Dr. Abby’s approach begins with the individual rather than the treatment. She considers skin history, goals, lifestyle and long-term maintenance before recommending a plan. For aesthetic treatments, she favors restraint and results that still look like the patient.
