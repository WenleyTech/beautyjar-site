About Amara

Amara Bennett, DNP, FNP-C, is a Family Nurse Practitioner and founder of Bennett Aesthetics + Wellness in Atlanta. Her practice focuses on aesthetic medicine and skin-focused care, with an emphasis on individualized treatment planning and natural-looking results.

Her BeautyJar Spotlight profile gives prospective patients a fuller view of her professional focus, treatment philosophy, credentials, work and practice.

Beyond the Practice

Outside the practice, Amara enjoys wellness travel, trying new Atlanta restaurants and spending time with family.

Her Approach

Amara’s approach begins with the individual rather than the treatment. She considers goals, health history, lifestyle and long-term maintenance before recommending a plan. For aesthetic treatments, she favors restraint and natural-looking results that still look like the patient.
