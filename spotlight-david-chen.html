About David

Start with the cause—not a single procedure.

David Chen is an NCCPA-certified Physician Assistant and founder of Chen Hair Restoration in Chicago. His practice focuses on hair restoration, PRP therapy and scalp health for patients experiencing pattern hair loss, thinning and early-stage alopecia.

He identifies the likely cause before recommending treatment. David combines PRP therapy, low-level laser treatment and surgical hair restoration when appropriate, while setting realistic timelines from the start.

Professional focus

Hair Restoration Surgery

Procedure planning and surgical restoration for appropriate candidates.

PRP Therapy

Platelet-rich plasma treatment used as part of an individualized hair-loss plan.

Scalp Health + LLLT

Scalp evaluation and low-level laser therapy based on pattern and stage.

Hair-restoration results take months, not weeks.
