About David

David Chen is an NCCPA-certified Physician Assistant and founder of Chen Hair Restoration in Chicago, Illinois. His practice focuses on hair restoration, PRP therapy and scalp health, working with patients experiencing pattern hair loss, thinning and early-stage alopecia.

David works from a diagnostic-first approach — identifying the underlying cause of hair loss before recommending treatment, rather than defaulting to a single procedure. He believes hair restoration should be evaluated the same way any other medical treatment is: by whether it actually works for the specific patient in front of him.

His Approach

David combines PRP therapy, low-level laser treatment and surgical hair restoration depending on what a patient’s hair-loss pattern and stage call for. He works with a collaborating dermatologist on surgical cases and is direct about setting realistic timelines — hair-restoration results take months, not weeks, and he would rather a patient know that upfront than be sold on speed.

Treatments

Hair Restoration Surgery

Procedure planning and surgical restoration for appropriate candidates.

PRP Therapy

Platelet-rich plasma treatment used as part of an individualized hair-loss plan.

Scalp evaluation and low-level laser therapy based on hair-loss pattern and stage.
