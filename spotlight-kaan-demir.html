About Dr. Demir

Dr. Kaan Demir is a hair restoration surgeon and founder of Demir Hair Restoration in New York City. He trained at leading hair-transplant clinics in Istanbul, a city widely regarded as a global center for hair-restoration technique, before focusing his practice specifically on female hair loss — a category he felt was consistently underserved, since most hair-restoration marketing and research has historically centered on male pattern baldness.

Dr. Demir works with patients experiencing alopecia areata, postpartum shedding, female pattern thinning and traction alopecia, using both surgical and non-surgical approaches depending on the diagnosis. He is direct that not every case of hair loss calls for a transplant, and that identifying the correct cause comes before recommending any procedure.

His Approach

Dr. Demir's consultations begin with a detailed diagnostic evaluation — scalp and follicle assessment, medical history and hormonal factors where relevant — before any treatment is discussed. For surgical candidates, he offers both FUE (follicular unit extraction) and DHI (direct hair implantation) techniques, selected based on donor density and the pattern of loss. For non-surgical cases, he coordinates with dermatology and endocrinology as needed rather than defaulting to a procedure that will not address the underlying cause.

Treatments

FUE Hair Transplant

Follicular unit extraction for candidates with suitable donor density.

DHI Hair Transplant

Direct hair implantation technique, often used for finer restoration work along the hairline.

Female Hair Loss Consultation

Diagnostic evaluation for alopecia, postpartum shedding and pattern thinning.
