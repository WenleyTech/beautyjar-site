About Dr. Demir

Diagnosis comes before procedure.

Dr. Kaan Demir is a hair restoration surgeon and founder of Demir Hair Restoration in New York City. After training at leading hair-transplant clinics in Istanbul, he focused his practice on female hair loss—an area historically underserved by hair-restoration marketing and research.

He works with alopecia areata, postpartum shedding, female pattern thinning and traction alopecia. Not every case calls for a transplant, so his consultations begin with scalp and follicle assessment, medical history and hormonal factors where relevant.

Professional focus

FUE Hair Transplant

Follicular unit extraction for candidates with suitable donor density.

DHI Hair Transplant

Direct hair implantation, often used for finer restoration work along the hairline.

Female Hair Loss

Diagnostic evaluation for alopecia, postpartum shedding and pattern thinning.

The right diagnosis matters more than selling a procedure.
